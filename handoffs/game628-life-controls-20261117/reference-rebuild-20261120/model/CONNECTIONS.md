# Reference street — declared interfaces before modeling

Meters, glTF Y up, +Z toward street, ground Y0. Five original illustrated shops are rebuilt as new geometry; no K files modified. No claim of literal pixel reproduction. Intentional view-dependent stage scene, not playable interiors/back elevations.

| ID | Expected relationship / opening | Inspection |
|---|---|---|
| ARCH-01 | Structural side piers, header, rear/side walls and floor seat with 10–30mm hidden insertion. Shopfronts genuinely open; no opaque glass sheets. | Front oblique / shop close / rear |
| WIN-02 | Upper large windows are actual holes in segmented wall; frames/reveals join surrounding plaster. Original broad one-residential-floor proportions above shops. No four-storey narrow windows. | Window close / side |
| BAL-03 | Balcony deck roots insert 30mm into facade; bars insert deck and rail at least 5mm. Open gaps between bars and behind windows intentional. | Balcony front/back / underside |
| CAN-04 | Closed cloth canopy touches wall rail; scalloped closed skirt overlaps front 15mm; support arms contact wall mounts and canopy. | Shop oblique / awning underside |
| FIT-05 | Shelves fixed to rear wall, shelves join sides; countertop sits on cabinet. Fruit/bread/utensils sit on trays or shelves, not floating. Open basket/crate tops intentional. | Each shop close |
| CAT-06 | Cat-tree posts enter each platform. Stylized cats' feet/body sit on platform or counter; ears/tails join body through hidden insertion. | Cat shop close |
| BIKE-07 | Static bicycle tubes meet hubs/lugs; spokes sit within rims, rim inside tire. Hanging wheel hooks anchored to rear panel. | Repair shop close |
| PLANT-08 | Stems/branches enter pot soil and leaf clusters. Tree branches intersect continuous trunk and terminate inside crown clusters. Gaps between leaves intentional, no shadow planes. | Tree/planter close |
| STREET-09 | Pavement blocks meet with intentional 5–8mm mortar joints over a closed foundation; tactile strip raised only 8mm. Bollards seat ground. | Low street oblique |
| LIGHT-10 | Pendant shade attached to cord and ceiling; bulb inside shade. Geometry only, very low emissive; no actual GLB lights or baked illumination. | Interior overhead |

Hidden penetrations are controlled joint insertions, not Boolean removal of all overlaps. Closed individual parts can remain separate. Floors and all visible solids have thickness. Cloth/curtains retain closed thickness; open store entrances, window holes, shelf bays, planter tops and bicycle spoke spaces are intentional. No extra approval or K-line changes implied. Final exported GLB must be reimported for geometry and visual checks; passing those is producer draft review, not user art acceptance.
